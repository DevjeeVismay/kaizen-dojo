## 🌏 1. Journey to Japan
**🧭 Question:** Where did the way of Kaizen originate?

## 👁️ 2. Training Begins: The Path of Observation
**🧘 Question:** What is the first step in true Kaizen training?  
✅ **Answer:** **A. Observing the process**  
> *Kaizen begins with understanding the current state before making changes.*

---

## 💪 3. 1st Strength: Kaizen is for Everyone
**👨‍🏫 Question:** Who is responsible for suggesting improvements in the Kaizen way?  
✅ **Answer:** **C. Everyone, from the noodle maker to the master**  
> *In Kaizen, improvement is everyone’s responsibility.*

---

## 🌱 4. 2nd Strength: Small Steps, Big Change
**⚙️ Question:** How does Kaizen achieve great results?  
✅ **Answer:** **B. By making small, consistent improvements**  
> *Continuous small improvements lead to long-term transformation.*

---

## 🌀 5. 3rd Strength: It is Easy to Do it Right
**📋 Question:** Why is it important that Kaizen focuses on simple, clear processes?  
✅ **Answer:** **A. So it's easy for everyone to follow the correct way**  
> *Simplicity ensures consistency and quality.*

---

## 🤝 6. 4th Strength: Everyone Improves Together
**👥 Question:** What is the outcome of this shared journey?  
✅ **Answer:** **B. It creates a culture of teamwork and shared success**  
> *When the team grows, everyone grows.*

---

## ⏳ 7. 1st Weakness: Results Can Feel Slow at First
**🐢 Question:** Why could this be a challenge for the Furious Five?  
✅ **Answer:** **A. They are used to seeing immediate, powerful results**  
> *Patience is required—Kaizen focuses on steady, sustainable progress.*

## 🧘 8. 2nd Weakness: Requires Great Discipline
**🧠 Question:** Why might this be difficult?

> *Discipline keeps the spirit of continuous improvement alive.*
